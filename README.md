# 📖 What Is CIPC?

The **Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC)** is the South African government authority responsible for registering companies and maintaining official business records.

When you **register a company online**, CIPC creates your company's legal identity and issues your official **company registration number**.

Without CIPC registration, your business is generally not recognised as a separate legal entity.

# 💼 CIPC vs SARS — What's the Difference?

Many new business owners confuse CIPC with SARS.

Although they work together, they have different responsibilities.

**CIPC** is responsible for legally registering your business.

**SARS** is responsible for managing your company's tax obligations.

After your **company registration CIPC** process is complete, your business is linked to SARS for income tax purposes. Depending on your business activities, additional registrations such as VAT, PAYE, or UIF may still be required.
